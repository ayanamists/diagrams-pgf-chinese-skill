# 19 UFIT

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11150893/

## Paper Context

UFIT integrates user-centered design, human factors/ergonomics, and implementation science to guide technology implementation. The framework centers users while accounting for organizational and implementation context.

## Figure Intent

Show a user-centered integration model. The viewer should see user needs at the center and surrounding theory/practice domains that inform implementation.

## Concepts To Show

- User or stakeholder needs
- User-centered design
- Human factors/ergonomics
- Implementation science
- Organizational context
- Technology fit
- Iterative evaluation

## Relationships

User needs sit at the center. UCD, human factors, and implementation science contribute methods around the center. Organizational context and technology fit influence implementation outcomes.

## Chinese Labels

Include labels such as `用户需求`, `以用户为中心`, `人因工程`, `实施科学`.

## Design Notes

Use a central node with surrounding domains and an outer context boundary. Avoid a flat list; the centrality of users is the point.
