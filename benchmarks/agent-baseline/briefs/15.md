# 15 EBM-DPSER

Source: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0070766

## Paper Context

The EBM-DPSER model links environmental drivers, pressures, states, ecosystem services, and responses. It adapts causal environmental reporting frameworks to emphasize ecosystem services and management response.

## Figure Intent

Show a causal chain with feedback. The viewer should understand that human drivers create pressures, pressures alter environmental state, state affects ecosystem services, and responses feed back into the system.

## Concepts To Show

- Drivers
- Pressures
- State
- Ecosystem services
- Human well-being or benefits
- Response/management
- Feedback loop

## Relationships

Drivers lead to pressures. Pressures change state. State affects services and benefits. Responses are informed by impacts and can act back on drivers, pressures, or state.

## Chinese Labels

Include labels such as `驱动`, `压力`, `状态`, `生态系统服务`, `响应`.

## Design Notes

Use a main left-to-right causal chain plus a curved feedback arrow from response back toward earlier stages.
