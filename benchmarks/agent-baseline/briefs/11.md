# 11 Raft

Source: https://raft.github.io/raft.pdf

## Paper Context

Raft is a consensus algorithm designed to be understandable. Servers move through follower, candidate, and leader roles; elections select a leader, and the leader replicates logs through heartbeats and append entries.

## Figure Intent

Show the server role state machine and the events that cause transitions. The viewer should understand timeout, election, vote, and heartbeat relationships.

## Concepts To Show

- Follower
- Candidate
- Leader
- Election timeout
- Request votes
- Majority vote
- Heartbeat or append entries
- Step down on higher term

## Relationships

Followers become candidates after timeout. Candidates become leaders after receiving a majority. Leaders maintain authority through heartbeats. Any server steps down when it observes a higher term.

## Chinese Labels

Include labels such as `跟随者`, `候选人`, `领导者`, `心跳`, `选举超时`.

## Design Notes

Use a state-machine triangle or cycle. Label transition arrows with events; otherwise the diagram looks like three unrelated circles.
