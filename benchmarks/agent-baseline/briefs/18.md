# 18 DTx RWE

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10951831/

## Paper Context

The Digital Therapeutics Real-World Evidence framework organizes evidence generation for digital therapeutics across a product lifecycle. It connects development, evaluation, deployment, monitoring, and decisions about evidence sufficiency.

## Figure Intent

Show a lifecycle, not a one-way checklist. The viewer should see decision/review points and feedback from real-world monitoring into future evidence generation.

## Concepts To Show

- Product design/development
- Clinical evaluation
- Deployment
- Real-world data collection
- Evidence review
- Regulatory or payer decision
- Post-market monitoring
- Feedback loop

## Relationships

Development leads to evaluation and deployment. Real-world data feeds evidence review. Review informs decisions and can send feedback to development or monitoring.

## Chinese Labels

Include labels such as `真实世界证据`, `生命周期`, `证据审查`, `监测`.

## Design Notes

Use a circular or looped lifecycle with explicit decision diamonds. A linear flow underplays the ongoing evidence cycle.
