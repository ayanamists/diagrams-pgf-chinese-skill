# 08 BERT

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04805

## Paper Context

BERT pre-trains a bidirectional Transformer encoder using masked language modeling and next-sentence prediction, then fine-tunes the same encoder for downstream NLP tasks.

## Figure Intent

Show two phases: pre-training with self-supervised objectives and fine-tuning with task-specific heads. The shared BERT encoder should be visually central.

## Concepts To Show

- Token, segment, and position embeddings
- Shared BERT encoder stack
- Masked language modeling head
- Next-sentence prediction head
- Fine-tuning task heads such as classification or question answering
- Pre-train to fine-tune transition

## Relationships

Embeddings feed the BERT encoder. During pre-training, the encoder feeds MLM and NSP objectives. During fine-tuning, the same encoder feeds task heads.

## Chinese Labels

Include labels such as `预训练`, `微调`, `遮蔽语言模型`, `句子关系`.

## Design Notes

Use a two-stage layout: pre-training on the left, fine-tuning on the right, with the shared encoder emphasized in both. Avoid representing BERT as a generic encoder-only box with no objectives.
