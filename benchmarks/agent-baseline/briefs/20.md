# 20 NASSS

Source: https://bmcmedicine.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12916-018-1050-6

## Paper Context

NASSS analyzes why technology-supported health or care innovations are not adopted, abandoned, or fail to scale, spread, and sustain. It considers multiple interacting domains rather than a single adoption barrier.

## Figure Intent

Show a multi-domain framework around scale-up and sustainability. The viewer should see several domains contributing complexity to the adoption outcome.

## Concepts To Show

- Condition
- Technology
- Value proposition
- Adopters
- Organization
- Wider system
- Embedding over time
- Scale-up, spread, and sustainability outcome

## Relationships

Each domain contributes uncertainty or complexity. The domains jointly shape whether adoption, scale-up, spread, and sustainability succeed. Time/embedding should be shown as a cross-cutting dimension.

## Chinese Labels

Include labels such as `规模化`, `可持续`, `采用者`, `组织`, `外部系统`.

## Design Notes

Use a hub-and-spoke or layered framework. Do not make it a causal pipeline; NASSS is a multi-domain diagnostic framework.
