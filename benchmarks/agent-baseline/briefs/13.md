# 13 PRISMA 2020

Source: https://journals.plos.org/plosmedicine/article/figure?id=10.1371/journal.pmed.1003583.g001

## Paper Context

PRISMA 2020 standardizes reporting for systematic reviews. Its flow diagram tracks records from identification through screening, eligibility assessment, and final inclusion.

## Figure Intent

Show attrition through review stages. The viewer should see where records enter, where duplicates or exclusions leave, and how final included studies are reached.

## Concepts To Show

- Identification
- Records found
- Duplicates removed
- Screening
- Reports assessed
- Excluded records/reports
- Included studies

## Relationships

Records flow downward through stages. Exclusion boxes branch to the side at screening and eligibility steps. The final included set sits at the bottom.

## Chinese Labels

Include labels such as `识别`, `筛选`, `排除`, `纳入研究`.

## Design Notes

Use a vertical flow with side branches for exclusions. Keep stage names and counts/placeholder counts visually separate.
