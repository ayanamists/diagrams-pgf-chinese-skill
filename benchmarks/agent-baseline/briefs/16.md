# 16 EGT

Source: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1175740/full

## Paper Context

Engaging in the Good with Technology frames technology use through positive engagement dimensions. The model connects technology practices to seeing, feeling, and doing good.

## Figure Intent

Show a triadic conceptual model around technology use. The viewer should see three mutually reinforcing dimensions rather than a linear process.

## Concepts To Show

- Technology use
- Seeing good
- Feeling good
- Doing good
- Personal or social context
- Reinforcing relationships between dimensions

## Relationships

Technology use is central. Seeing, feeling, and doing good surround it and interact with each other. Context can frame the entire model.

## Chinese Labels

Include labels such as `技术使用`, `看见善`, `感受善`, `实践善`.

## Design Notes

Use a triangle or radial layout with technology use at the center. Avoid making it a step-by-step pipeline.
