# 07 U-Net

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.04597

## Paper Context

U-Net is an encoder-decoder convolutional architecture for biomedical image segmentation. It contracts spatial resolution to capture context, then expands resolution while using skip connections to recover localization detail.

## Figure Intent

Show the U-shaped topology: downsampling path, bottleneck, upsampling path, and skip connections between matching resolutions.

## Concepts To Show

- Input image
- Contracting/downsampling blocks
- Bottleneck
- Expanding/upsampling blocks
- Skip connections
- Segmentation map output
- Increasing channels during contraction and decreasing channels during expansion

## Relationships

The left side progressively reduces spatial resolution. The right side restores resolution. Skip connections bridge left and right blocks at matching scales.

## Chinese Labels

Include labels such as `下采样`, `瓶颈`, `跳跃连接`, `分割输出`.

## Design Notes

Use a clear U shape. Avoid cluttering the diagram with every convolution; showing scale levels and skip links is more important than drawing many tiny blocks.
