# 05 Transformer

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762

## Paper Context

The Transformer replaces recurrent sequence processing with attention-based encoder and decoder stacks. Each stack combines multi-head attention, feed-forward layers, residual connections, normalization, and positional information.

## Figure Intent

Show the encoder-decoder architecture and make attention the central operation. The figure should communicate that the decoder attends both to prior output tokens and to encoder representations.

## Concepts To Show

- Input tokens and embeddings
- Positional encoding
- Encoder stack
- Multi-head self-attention
- Feed-forward layer
- Decoder stack
- Encoder-decoder attention
- Output probabilities

## Relationships

Inputs enter the encoder. Decoder inputs are shifted output tokens. Encoder representations feed a cross-attention block inside the decoder. Decoder output feeds a linear/softmax prediction stage.

## Chinese Labels

Include labels such as `自注意力`, `编码器`, `解码器`, `位置编码`.

## Design Notes

Use two tall columns for encoder and decoder with internal repeated blocks. Keep arrows clean: input bottom-to-top or left-to-right, and one clear cross-attention connection between columns.
