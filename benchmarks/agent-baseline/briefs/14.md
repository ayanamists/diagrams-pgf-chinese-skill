# 14 CONSORT 2010

Source: https://journals.plos.org/plosmedicine/article?id=10.1371/journal.pmed.1000251

## Paper Context

CONSORT 2010 standardizes reporting for randomized trials. Its participant flow diagram tracks enrollment, allocation, follow-up, and analysis for each trial arm.

## Figure Intent

Show participants moving from eligibility assessment into randomization and then into parallel intervention arms. The figure should emphasize balanced reporting across arms.

## Concepts To Show

- Assessed for eligibility
- Excluded before randomization
- Randomized
- Allocation to arm A and arm B
- Follow-up losses
- Analysis sets

## Relationships

Participants flow downward to randomization, split into arms, continue through follow-up, then analysis. Exclusions and losses branch sideways from the main flow.

## Chinese Labels

Include labels such as `合格评估`, `随机分配`, `随访`, `分析`.

## Design Notes

Use a vertical trunk that splits into two balanced columns. Symmetry matters because the figure compares reporting completeness across arms.
